## 1. Mandatory Data Attributions

As specified in competition requirements and data licensing terms, the following attributions apply to all maps, figures, reports, and UI dashboards generated by FloodShield:

### Copernicus Sentinel Satellite Data
> **Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026.**
- Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Ground Range Detected (GRD) and Single Look Complex (SLC) products.
- Sentinel-2 MultiSpectral Instrument (MSI) Level-2A bottom-of-atmosphere reflectance data.
- Accessed via Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE) and AWS Open Data (Earth Search by Element 84).

### Copernicus WorldDEM-30 (GLO-30)
> **Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.**
- 30-meter global Digital Elevation Model used for terrain slope correction, radar layover/shadow masking, and D8 downstream drainage path tracing.

### OpenStreetMap (OSM)
> **© OpenStreetMap contributors.**
- Historical infrastructure geometries (road network ways, building footprints, bridges, and settlement nodes) extracted strictly prior to event dates via the HeiGIT **ohsome API** (OpenStreetMap History Data).
- OpenStreetMap data is licensed under the [Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/) by the OpenStreetMap Foundation (OSMF).
- Any extracted historical OSM geometries distributed as part of data exports remain subject to the ODbL.

### Copernicus Emergency Management Service (Reference Validation Only)
> **European Union, Copernicus Emergency Management Service data**
- Copernicus EMS Rapid Mapping Activation [EMSR927](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR927/) (Trishuli Basin Flood Event, August 2026).
- **Strict Quarantine Rule:** EMSR927 products are strictly sequestered for post-inference independent validation and are never used as training data, feature inputs, or threshold calibration sources.
